- Normal Free CC eligibility requires at least $100 in positive paid deposits during the current UTC calendar month.
- VIP status requires at least $500 in positive paid deposits during the current UTC calendar month.
- Eligible normal users may claim one Free CC per UTC day; VIP users may claim two.
- The claim pool is all available unsold cards, not a separately allocated or replenished daily pool.
