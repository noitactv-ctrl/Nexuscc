---
name: Telegram daily rewards
description: Keep the paid daily-rewards campaign separate from Telegram free-credit drops.
---

The NexusCC daily-rewards campaign pays 25 cents per qualifying daily claim. A referred user earns 10 cents extra per qualifying day, and their linked referrer earns 10 cents for each day that referred user qualifies. Eligibility requires joining the configured channel and keeping `NexusCC.cc` in the Telegram display name. Wallet payouts use a one-time code generated from the signed-in website profile; do not trust an email or username alone to link an account.

**Why:** This follows the requested daily/referral reward model while protecting website balances and preserving the separate chat-only free-credit drop system.

**How to apply:** Keep daily claims, referral attribution, and wallet links in their own records and transactions. Do not repurpose the free-credit claim/referral tables or require store-account linking to claim drops.
