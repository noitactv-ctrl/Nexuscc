---
name: Telegram daily rewards
description: Keep the paid daily-rewards campaign separate from Telegram free-credit drops.
---

The OmniMarket daily-rewards campaign automatically pays $1.00 per qualifying day. A referred user earns 10 cents extra per qualifying day, and their linked referrer earns 10 cents for each day that referred user qualifies. Eligibility requires joining the configured channel and keeping `OmniMarket` in the Telegram display name. Wallet payouts use a one-time code generated from the signed-in website profile; do not trust an email or username alone to link an account.

Users may share their daily referral link after passing the channel and display-name gates, before linking their website account. Account linking remains required to receive daily rewards and referral payouts.

**Why:** The user requested share-before-linking and set the base daily reward to $1.00; linked accounts remain necessary for balance payouts, and the free-credit drop system remains separate.

**How to apply:** Keep daily claims, referral attribution, and wallet links in their own records and transactions. Do not repurpose the free-credit claim/referral tables or require store-account linking to claim drops.
